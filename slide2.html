Main observation

The booking path feels “disconnected” because guests move across multiple pages and a separate booking subdomain. When availability is low or rates aren’t clear, there is no strong guided next step (alternate dates, other room types, offers, or an easy “call/WhatsApp to book” fallback).

What to fix first

  • Reduce friction: minimize redirects and keep guests oriented with a consistent booking header + CTAs.
  • International readiness: add multi-language and multi-currency support (so overseas guests don’t bounce).
  • Trust & parity: show reviews, offers, and a price-check to counter “OTA is cheaper” assumptions.
  • Fallbacks when sold out: smarter suggestions instead of a dead-end calendar.

Current booking journey (today)

Step 1 — Group website Guest discovers brand, then must find the property page before seeing rates/availability.
Step 2 — Property page More scrolling + clicks to reach booking. CTAs can be clearer and more persistent.
Step 3 — Booking subdomain Different UI/branding + limited language/currency options can reduce trust and conversion.

RateMatch approach

Keep your brand website intact, but simplify the booking path and add conversion tools: multi-language, multi-currency, price-checker, reviews, offers, and a smoother mobile checkout.
